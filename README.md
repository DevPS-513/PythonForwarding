## Is Jira Free to Use?

Yes, Jira offers a free tier with the following options:

### Jira Free Plan
- **Price**: Free forever
- **Users**: Up to 10 users
- **Features**:
  - Scrum and Kanban boards
  - Backlog and roadmap planning
  - Agile reporting
  - 2GB storage
  - Community support

### Paid Plans
For teams larger than 10 users or requiring advanced features, Atlassian offers:
- **Standard**: Starts at $7.75/user/month
- **Premium**: Starts at $15.25/user/month
- **Enterprise**: Custom pricing for large organizations

The free tier is suitable for small teams and provides core project management functionality. For more information, visit the [official Atlassian pricing page](https://www.atlassian.com/software/jira/pricing).
